# Personal Archive

A personal creative space for photographs, writing, and experimental work. It is an authored archive, not a hiring-focused portfolio.

## Language

**Archive**:
The privacy-conscious collection of photographs, organised into non-identifying themes.
_Avoid_: Gallery, travel feed, photo dump

**Archive Theme**:
An evocatively named group of photographs in the Archive that does not reveal precise place or date information.
_Avoid_: Album, trip, location

**Note**:
A lightweight, dateable Markdown entry combining a title, preview, tags, and optionally featured artwork.
_Avoid_: Blog post, article

**Work**:
The loose creative lab of projects and experiments shown on the site.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, case-study collection

**Project Story**:
A short Work detail page containing an overview, role and tools, selected visuals, and relevant links.
_Avoid_: Case study
